Act as a growth strategist, behavioral psychologist, viral product expert, and social UX designer.

We are building a mobile app that should achieve explosive organic growth through user-driven sharing.

Your task is to design a complete "Emotional Viral System" that encourages users to share their personal experience, emotional state, and identity in a natural, authentic, and non-cringey way.

---

## 1. PERSONAL EMOTIONAL FINGERPRINT (CORE VIRAL MECHANISM)

Design a system where each user has a unique “emotional fingerprint” that can be shared externally.

Examples:
- daily emotional summary
- mood aura / energy state
- personal insight
- emotional score or pattern
- “who I am today” snapshot

The fingerprint must be:
- visually beautiful
- minimal
- meaningful
- shareable as an image/story
- not exposing sensitive data directly

---

## 2. SHAREABLE CONTENT DESIGN

Design multiple share formats:

- Instagram story format (vertical, premium look)
- Snapchat-friendly quick share
- WhatsApp card
- Twitter/X post format

Each should include:
- subtle branding (not intrusive)
- emotional message
- personal insight
- visually attractive gradients / motion

---

## 3. SMART COPYWRITING (CRITICAL)

Define the exact tone of messages that appear when sharing:

The tone must be:
- deep
- slightly mysterious
- emotionally intelligent
- positive but not fake
- reflective
- not cheesy

Examples:
- “Today, I chose calm over chaos.”
- “Not everything heavy needs to be carried.”
- “Some days, growth is just surviving quietly.”

---

## 4. TRIGGER MOMENTS FOR SHARING

Define when the app prompts sharing:

- after emotional insight
- after completing a session (breathing / reflection)
- after progress milestone
- after a “breakthrough moment”

IMPORTANT:
- never interrupt
- always feel natural
- optional, never forced

---

## 5. VIRAL LOOP DESIGN

Define how sharing leads to growth:

- shared content sparks curiosity
- viewer clicks → landing page / app download
- onboarding references the shared content
- new user creates their own fingerprint
- loop repeats

---

## 6. SOCIAL PSYCHOLOGY MECHANICS

Use:
- identity expression
- curiosity gap
- subtle exclusivity
- emotional relatability
- aesthetic appeal

Avoid:
- spammy invites
- forced referrals
- fake rewards

---

## 7. ADVANCED VIRAL FEATURES

Design additional ideas:

- “Compare emotional journeys” (optional, safe)
- anonymous community highlights
- trending emotional states (aggregated)
- shareable weekly reports
- streak-based identity (“7 days of calm”)

---

## 8. PRIVACY-FIRST SHARING

Define:
- what is safe to share
- what must never be shared
- user control levels

---

## OUTPUT:

A) Emotional fingerprint system  
B) Shareable design formats  
C) Copywriting system  
D) Viral loop architecture  
E) Trigger strategy  
F) Privacy rules  
G) Growth strategy roadmap
